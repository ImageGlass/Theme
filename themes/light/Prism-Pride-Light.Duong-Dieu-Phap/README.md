# Prism Pride Light

Prism Pride Light pairs hot pink outlines with warm gray details on a soft rose canvas. The toolbar and gallery share a translucent rose tint, while the viewer lets just a little of the window backdrop through. The familiar ImageGlass logo keeps its white ig lettering over six solid Pride stripes.

Author: Duong Dieu Phap

## Install in ImageGlass 10

1. Open Settings (Ctrl+,), then Appearance → Theme.
2. Click Install theme packs and select the .igtheme.zip file.
3. Select Prism Pride Light for Light mode, then click Apply or OK.
4. Use the matching app color mode to see the theme.

## Artwork

- 35 original SVG toolbar icons on a 24 × 24 grid, with rounded joins and caps.
- Consistent 1.25-unit strokes, with approximately 70% hot pink and 30% warm gray across the toolbar artwork.
- The official ImageGlass logo geometry, recolored with six solid Pride stripes. The original outline and ig lettering are preserved exactly, with no gradients, shadows or outer padding in the 48 × 48 viewBox.
- A 1200 × 760 WebP preview, with its editable SVG source.
- Self-contained artwork: no icon fonts, linked images or third-party icon libraries.

Viewer: #FBF3F7E6 · Toolbar: #fae1eeb0 · Gallery: #fae1eeb0 · Menu: #faebf3
Text: #7d003f · Accent: #E6007E

A custom viewer background in ImageGlass settings can override the theme background.
The viewer uses approximately 10% transparency (alpha E6). Toolbar and gallery share #fae1eeb0, with approximately 31% transparency. All three blend with the window backdrop.
The preview presents the theme name, palette, official logo and two rows of icon samples. Its icon panel shows the toolbar tint composited over the standard active-window fallback.
